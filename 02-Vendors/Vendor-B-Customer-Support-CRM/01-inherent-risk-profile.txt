# Inherent-risk profile
**Vendor (simulated):** Northline Support Cloud  
**Service:** Hosted customer-support CRM  
**Track:** A — new vendor  
**Date:** 1 September 2026  
**Assessor:** TPRM (portfolio sample)

## Relationship
Northline stores customer name, address, phone, email, and member /
account identifiers. Agents can view tickets. Free-text tickets may
include extra sensitive notes. Northline personnel can access records.
Service is multi-tenant SaaS with more than one subprocessor.

## Inherent scoring

| Factor | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Data | 3 | Customer PII; possible sensitive ticket notes |
| Access | 2 | Vendor staff can view production tickets |
| Criticality | 2 | Primary support channel; not a payment rail |
| 4th party / hosting | 3 | Cloud SaaS; multiple subprocessors |
| **Average** | **2.5** | **High** |

## Decision
High tier. Full information-security assessment. Information Security
sign-off required. Site visit not scheduled in this sample because
the service is multi-tenant SaaS; evidence review substitutes unless
policy requires an on-site for High PII hosts.

## Next step
NDA confirmed. Vendor assessment questionnaire plus SOC 2, ISO or
equivalent, pentest attestation, complete subprocessor list, BCP,
insurance. A CAIQ or SIG would be requested in a live High-tier
cloud review; not attached in this repo.
